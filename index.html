Queries & mapping

Where the software is unsure, it asks rather than guesses. Answer a query and the mapping is applied and remembered. You can also extend the chart of accounts with your own rules and captions.

Adjusting journal entries

Post an adjustment here rather than editing the trial balance. The imported figures stay untouched as your unadjusted position, and every statement, note and schedule is prepared on the adjusted balances. Nothing is applied until an entry balances.

Financial statements

Adjust and finalise

The prepared statements are never altered. Anything you change here sits on top of them as your adjustment, and the finalised version is what gets exported. Every line shows what was prepared beside what you made it, and any line reverts on its own.

Audit checks

Materiality, analytical review, exception reports and Nepal-specific compliance tests, run over the mapped trial balance and the schedules. Everything here is evidence to follow up, not an opinion.
